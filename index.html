Programs

Three ways to start the year

Every schedule includes the same core curriculum. Pick the length of day that fits your family.

8:30 – 12:00

Half-day

Literacy, math, and outdoor play, finishing before lunch. A gentle first step for children new to a full school routine.

8:30 – 3:00

Full-day

The complete program with lunch, quiet reading, art, and music. Most of our families choose this schedule.

7:30 – 6:00

Full-day plus extended care

Full-day program with supervised morning and afternoon care for working parents.

A day here

What a full day looks like

The rhythm stays the same each day, so children know what comes next.

  1. 8:15
    Arrival and free choicePuzzles, blocks, and books while friends trickle in.
  2. 8:45
    Morning circleCalendar, weather, songs, and the plan for the day.
  3. 9:15
    Reading and writingLetter sounds, first words, and a daily page in a journal.
  4. 10:15
    Snack and outdoor playGarden beds, climbing frame, and open field.
  5. 11:00
    Math workshopCounting, patterns, and shapes with real objects.
  6. 12:00
    Lunch and recessChildren eat together, then run it off.
  7. 12:45
    Quiet readingRest, story time, or looking at picture books alone.
  8. 1:15
    Art, music, or scienceA different studio each weekday.
  9. 2:30
    Pack up and closing circleEach child shares one thing they learned.
  10. 3:00
    DismissalPickup at the front gate.
