Privacy notice

Last updated October 30, 2026. This notice explains what Little Oak Kindergarten collects when you register a child or request a tour.

What we collect

  • Your child's name, date of birth, program, and any allergies or health needs you list.
  • Your name, phone number, and email, and an emergency contact's name and phone number.
  • For tours, your name, email, and any note you add.

How we use it

We use this information only to enroll your child, contact your family, schedule tours, and keep your child safe at school. We do not sell it.

Payments

Payments are handled by Stripe on its own secure checkout page. We never see or store your card or bank details. Your registration details are saved with the payment record in our Stripe account.

Live chat

If you use the chat box on our website, the messages you type, and basic details such as your browser and approximate location, are handled by our chat provider, Tawk.to. Please do not put health details or payment information in chat.

Who can see it

Only school staff who need it, and our payment provider. Health notes are shared with the teachers who care for your child.

Your choices

You can ask us to correct or delete your information, subject to records we must keep. Contact the school office using the details on our website.
